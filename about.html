Our story

Your accounts are in safe hands

Kirpa Accountancy & Advisory was founded to give small and growing businesses the kind of financial support usually reserved for much larger companies.

Too many business owners only hear from their accountant once a year, when the deadline is looming. We take a different approach. By looking after the day-to-day (bookkeeping, payroll, VAT and the purchase ledger) we build a real understanding of your business. That lets us offer meaningful advice through management accounts, forecasts and budgets, all year round.

The name Kirpa reflects the spirit in which we work: with care, generosity and integrity. We treat every client's business as if it were our own, and we measure our success by yours.

Meet Anoop.

I'm Anoop, founder of Kirpa Accountancy & Advisory. I have over 23 years of finance experience across industry, international accounting firms and the charity sector. That range means I understand how very different organisations work and what they need from their numbers.

I'm a Fellow of the ACCA (FCCA) and hold ATT, AAT and ICB qualifications, including the ICB Level 3 Diploma in Payroll Management. My background covers financial control, audit, management accounting, VAT compliance and payroll.

I set up Kirpa to give small businesses, contractors and landlords the senior-level expertise larger organisations rely on, without the corporate price tag or the jargon.
