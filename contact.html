FAQs

Common questions

How much do your services cost?

We agree a fixed monthly fee based on the services you need and the size of your business. You'll receive a clear written proposal before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Can I switch to you from my current accountant?

Yes. Switching is straightforward and we handle most of it for you, including contacting your previous accountant for the information we need.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work with the leading cloud accounting platforms and can either work within your existing system or recommend and set up one that suits your business.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. We work with clients across the UK, with meetings by video call or phone and documents shared securely online.

Can you help if my books are behind?

Absolutely. We regularly help clients catch up on overdue bookkeeping and filings, and can liaise with HMRC on your behalf where needed.
